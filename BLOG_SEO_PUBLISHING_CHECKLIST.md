# Reusable SEO Article Publishing Checklist

## Before writing

- Identify search intent: informational, commercial, transactional, or comparison.
- Choose one primary focus keyword and a small set of genuinely related terms.
- Confirm the article adds original value and does not duplicate a product description.
- Verify factual, health, scientific, and sourcing claims against reliable sources.

## In the editor

- Write a unique title, clean slug, excerpt, SEO title, and meta description.
- Add one H1 only; organize the body with descriptive H2 and H3 headings.
- Add a descriptive featured-image alt text and an Open Graph image.
- Select a relevant category and tags; complete the author role and biography.
- Add descriptive links to relevant products, categories, related articles, About, certificates, wholesale, or contact pages.
- Add visible FAQs only when they help readers; never add FAQ schema for hidden or nonexistent FAQs.
- Set publish/schedule dates accurately and use the revision date only for a meaningful update.
- Keep “Index and follow” enabled only for original, complete, published content.

## Quality review

- Read the article for clarity, short paragraphs, accuracy, and keyword stuffing.
- Confirm health content has an informational disclaimer and appropriate sources.
- Confirm all images have meaningful alt text and all links work.
- Preview on mobile and check heading order, tables, lists, and image layout.

## After publishing

- Open the article without client-side JavaScript and confirm the main text remains visible.
- Check title, description, canonical, robots, one H1, structured data, and breadcrumbs.
- Confirm inclusion in `/blog-sitemap.xml` and absence from the sitemap when no-indexed.
- Inspect the URL in Google Search Console and submit the sitemap after deployment.
- Monitor indexing, impressions, clicks, CTR, queries, and broken links.
- Review important articles periodically and set a revision date only after substantive changes.
